Age Policy — 21 and Over Only

You must be 21 or older to buy anything from Mile High Smoke Shop & Convenience. This applies in store, by phone, for local delivery, and for shipped items. No exceptions.

Valid ID is required

We require a valid, government-issued photo ID showing that you are at least 21 years old:

  • In store: at the counter before purchase.
  • Local delivery: the person receiving the order must show valid ID to the driver, and the name should match the order. The driver checks ID at the door.
  • Shipped items: eligible shipments are sent with an adult-signature (21+) requirement where available.

We will refuse the sale

We will not complete a sale or delivery if a valid ID is not shown, if we believe the ID is false, if we believe the products are being bought for someone under 21, or if the buyer appears intoxicated. A driver may return an order to the store if age cannot be verified.

No sales to minors; no straw purchases

Selling or providing these products to anyone under 21 is illegal, and so is buying them on behalf of someone under 21. We take this seriously to protect our customers, our community, and our business.

Age gate

This website asks you to confirm your age before entering. Confirming is not a substitute for showing ID; ID is always checked before an order is handed over.

Haga su pedido con anticipación y recójalo en 7400 Southland Blvd #113, Orlando, FL 32809, abierto todos los días de 10am a 11pm. Traiga una identificación válida de 21+.

2. Entrega local

Los pedidos enviados van a adultos de 21 años o más, con requisito de firma de un adulto donde esté disponible. Los tiempos y costos de envío se confirman al hacer el pedido.

Por razones de salud y seguridad, los productos de edad restringida y consumibles son, por lo general, venta final y no se pueden devolver una vez que salen de la tienda. Si un artículo está defectuoso o recibió el artículo equivocado, contáctenos de inmediato al (689) 233-8293 y lo resolveremos.
